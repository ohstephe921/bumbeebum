Games Grown-ups Help

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Bumbeebum makes learning games for young children. Our games are built so that they do not need to know anything about the people who play them. This policy covers all Bumbeebum games, including Luma's Star Quest.

What we collect

Nothing. Our games do not collect, transmit or share any personal information. They do not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

To remember your child's progress, a game saves the following on the device only:

  • which stages have been finished and the stars earned;
  • treasures found, and the dress, pet, glow and trail chosen;
  • which words have been learned;
  • the first name typed in for the "write your name" stage, if one is entered;
  • whether sound is switched on.

This information never leaves the device. Deleting the game deletes it.

Advertising, tracking and purchases

Our games contain no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. The only link out of a game is to our own website, bumbeebum.com. It sits in the grown-ups area, behind a parent gate, where children can't reach it.

Children's privacy

Because our games collect no information from anyone, they collect no information from children.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. If you email us, we use your address only to reply.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to christine@bumbeebum.com.
